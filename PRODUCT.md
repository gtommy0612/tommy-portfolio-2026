## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: hiring managers and design leads screening for a **Senior Product Designer**. They skim fast, usually on desktop between other candidates, and need to see impact, judgment and process within seconds before deciding to open a case study or the resume.

Secondary (not served by the homepage): freelance clients, who land on `services/` and `services/zh/`.

## Product Purpose

Tommy Chi's personal portfolio. Success = a hiring manager understands within one viewport what Tommy does, believes it with real numbers, opens a case study, and reaches out or downloads the resume.

## Positioning

Recommended from the resume (user asked for a recommendation; confirm before treating as final):
**A designer who turns ambiguous, regulated problems into shippable systems, fast, by using AI to compress concept time and design tokens to compress build time.**
The proof chain no neighbouring portfolio can copy: a $1.6M+ US state government bid won with a prototype delivered in 48 hours, concept time −60% (became team SOP), front-end build time −67% via engineering-aligned tokens, a public records flow cut 6 → 3 steps at 100% WCAG 2.2 AA. PMP® adds delivery credibility.

## Operating Context

Remote work between Taiwan and US stakeholders (Logikco, Feb 2023–present). Domains: US state government portals, enterprise SaaS, manufacturing data systems (WormHole Tech, 2021–2023). Tools: Claude, Figma Variables, HTML/CSS/JS, Git.

## Product Principles

1. Numbers before adjectives: every claim on the homepage is backed by a resume metric.
2. Show the work doing its job: real screens from the case studies, not decoration.
3. One viewport to decide: role, positioning and proof are legible before scrolling.
4. Hiring-manager speed: case studies and resume are always one click away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Not declared a binding constraint by the user, but the portfolio sells WCAG 2.2 AA expertise, so the site should not contradict it (contrast, focus, semantics).
